Privacy Policy

Synology Rclone Backup is a personal application used to back up Google Drive files to the owner's private Synology NAS.

Data Access and Use

The application requests read-only access to Google Drive files. The data is used exclusively for creating local backup copies.

Data Storage and Sharing

Backup copies are stored on the owner's private NAS. The application does not sell Google user data or share it with external users.

Retention and Deletion

Backup retention is managed locally by the NAS owner. The intended retention policy keeps the latest 10 daily backups and one monthly backup for up to 12 months.

The owner can delete backup files from the NAS and revoke OAuth access in Google Account settings.

Security

Google OAuth credentials are stored privately on the NAS and are not published on this website.
